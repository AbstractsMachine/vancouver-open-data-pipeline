# Weekly report — week of 7–13 September 2026

A public-money data pipeline for the City of Vancouver: everything the City
publishes about what it spends, owes, pays and builds, loaded, checked and
turned into open outputs. This repository holds the pipeline; the data itself
is downloaded from the public sources by the scripts.

## Built this week

**Ingestion**
- 36 datasets from the City's open-data portal, config-only (`config/vancouver.yaml`), with a catalog snapshot recording each dataset's licence and last update.
- The City's Statements of Financial Information 2011–2025 (17 PDFs), fetched from the Internet Archive because vancouver.ca blocks scripts. Each file's capture, original URL and SHA-256 is recorded.

**Parsing the PDFs**
- Payments to suppliers over $25,000 and grants by category: 19,330 supplier lines (one amount unreadable in the PDF) and 10,064 grant lines, 2011–2025.
- The five-year statistical review (revenue, expenses by service and by kind of cost, capital additions, debt, reserves, balance sheet, property tax): 3,835 values from 7 reports, 2015–2025.
- Every schedule is added up and compared with the total the City printed.

**Transformations (dbt, BigQuery)**
- 91 models (staging → analytics → marts) covering budget, debt and reserves, payments and grants, payroll, contracts and bids, capital budgets 2021–2026 and the 2023–2026 Capital Plan, City places, non-market housing, council decisions, building permits, and the city's physical fabric.
- 6,953 named payees (organisations and public bodies) with year-by-year history, linked to the contracts register where the names match.

**Measured and derived data**
- Building heights for the City's 154,124 building footprints, measured from national LiDAR (NRCan HRDEM 2016), including tower-on-podium shapes.
- 7,383 council decisions since 2016; 1,716 placed on the parcels their agenda text names (exact address, range, or same hundred block — no fuzzy matching).
- 1,096 City places (community centres, libraries, fire halls, parks, cultural spaces, shelters, non-market housing) tied to their parcel, the council decisions and permits on it, and the capital programs that name them.
- The City's address register (99,744 address points) joined to the city blocks and summarised as 14,227 house-number ranges — one per street, side of the street (odd or even numbers) and block — over 897 streets and 4,591 of the 4,633 blocks, so any address can be found on its block.

**Outputs**
- Council decisions as a downloadable dataset: 7,383 items (2016–2026) with outcome, tally and keyword subjects, plus 3,929 item × parcel rows, as gzipped CSVs with a column-by-column schema and a stats file every published count is read from.
- Spending per resident per month, for every year of the budget output.

## Data-quality findings

- **Contracts:** the register repeats one award amount beside several winners in 18 competitions ($128 M). The total is now counted once: $1.85 B instead of $1.98 B.
- **Misprints in the City's reports:** the 2023–2025 five-year tables shift capital additions by one column; the reports' own text confirms the correct figures.
- **Schedules:** of the printed totals checked, all agree within $50 except six, each traced and documented:
  - a truncated amount in the 2021 PDF (Aramark, ",541,678");
  - a typo in 2018 ("15,0000"), repaired;
  - three schedules whose lines were all read but still differ from the printed total;
  - two 2016 grant categories with no printed total.
- **Housing register:** two unit counts that disagree for 36 buildings. Both are kept.
- **Address register:** 78 address points sit on the shared edge of two block outlines, and a plain point-in-polygon join counted them twice (99,822 rows from 99,744 points). Each is now kept once, on the smaller block, and a test checks it. 1,196 points carry no street name and 459 fall outside every block; they stay in the analytics table and out of the ranges.
- **Repository:** the raw-source schema setting in `dbt/models/staging/sources.yml` carried escaped quotes and would not have resolved; fixed.
- Full list: [docs/data-notes.md](../docs/data-notes.md).

## Privacy rules applied

- The remuneration schedule and the payment schedules contain people's names. Names stay in the warehouse. Outputs publish counts and totals only.
- Medians and pay-band totals are withheld for groups under 10 people.
- A payee name that could be a person's is counted in every total but not published.

## Checks

- Every section of the five-year review adds up to its printed total, year by year.
- Revenue minus expenses equals the printed surplus.
- The two expense cuts (by service, by kind of cost) agree.
- The debt table's arithmetic holds.
- Contract totals agree across grains.
- Capital budget and plan totals survive from the raw files to the marts.
- The privacy rules hold.
- An address point joins at most one block.
- All data tests passed on the last full build (82); the four added since, on the address models, pass.
